# 📞 Sallah Call Center Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to monitor and evaluate the operational performance of a call center. The dashboard provides actionable insights into call handling efficiency, service quality, agent performance, and customer service metrics to support data-driven decision-making.

The dashboard helps answer key business questions such as:

- Are service level targets being achieved?
- Which periods experience the highest call volume?
- How efficiently are calls being handled?
- Which agents require performance improvement?
- Are forecasting and staffing aligned with customer demand?

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall call center performance.
- Track service quality and operational efficiency.
- Measure agent performance.
- Identify peak demand periods.
- Improve workforce planning.
- Reduce customer waiting time and abandoned calls.

# 📈 Executive Summary

- March recorded the highest call volume (~760K calls), indicating a significant increase in customer demand.
- Handling Rate remained above **97%** across all months, demonstrating consistently high operational efficiency.
- March also experienced the highest Abandon Rate (**2.15%**), suggesting peak demand affected customer waiting times.
- More than **92%** of calls were handled within the target threshold, reflecting strong SLA compliance.
- The Average Speed of Answer (ASA) remained at **9 seconds**, ensuring customers were connected quickly.
- Monday achieved the highest Handling Rate, while Saturday showed the lowest performance, indicating opportunities to optimize staffing schedules.
- Forecasted and Offered Calls followed similar trends throughout the reporting period, suggesting effective workforce planning.
- Despite the increase in call volume during March, service quality remained stable, demonstrating efficient resource management.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

- Increase staffing levels during high-demand periods, especially in March.
- Review the causes of higher abandon rates during peak call volumes.
- Optimize workforce scheduling for lower-performing weekdays.
- Continue maintaining ASA below 10 seconds to enhance customer experience.
- Improve workforce forecasting accuracy to better align staffing with demand.
- Provide coaching for agents with lower handling performance or higher abandon rates.
